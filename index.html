Day by Day

Five legs,
six unforgettable stops

01
Sun 7 June

Agkistri

Kalamaki → Skala

Depart 07:30, sail southwest past Aegina. First anchorage in the crystal-clear bay at Skala. Pine trees, quiet village, perfect introduction to the Saronic.

Swimming
Skala Beach ↗ — anchor off and swim straight from the boat
Aponisos Cove ↗ — secluded, rocky, exceptional clarity
Dinner
Telchines ↗★ 4.4
Waterfront under the trees · live Greek music nights · fresh fish · warm host
Quattro RestoBar ↗★ 4.5
Tables at the water's edge from 20:00 · best sunset spot · open until 03:00
~22nm · 4hrs
02
Mon 8 June

Poros

Agkistri → Poros

Sail south past Methana. Poros sits in a dramatic 200m-wide strait — the Peloponnese is right there. Walk up to the clock tower; water taxi to the lemon forest. Good provisioning stop.

Swimming
Russian Bay ↗ — sheltered, pine-fringed, perfect lunch hook
Askeli Beach ↗ — sandy, organised, easy by dinghy
Dinner
Taverna Apagio ↗★ 4.8
Family-run waterfront institution · black risotto, sea bass · reserve ahead
Oceanis Rooftop Garden ↗★ 4.9
Highest-rated in Poros · sunset cocktails · most romantic spot in town
~18nm · 3.5hrs
03
Tue 9 June

Spetses

Poros → Spetses

Longer passage south. Car-free island — horse carriages only. Beautiful old harbour, elegant mansions. Swim stop at Agioi Anargyri on the way in: the finest beach in the Saronic.

Swimming
Agioi Anargyri ↗ — finest beach in the Saronic · anchor for lunch
Zogeria Bay ↗ — secluded north-coast cove · crystal clear · forest backdrop
Dinner
Mourayo ↗★ 4.4
Sit at sea level in the Old Harbour · monkfish, octopus, eggplant rolls
Mosquito ↗★ 4.6
Locals' favourite · best food on the island · creative, handmade, lively
~22nm · 4hrs
04
Wed 10 June

Hydra ★

Spetses → Hydra

Depart Spetses 08:00 — arrive before noon to secure a stern-to berth. No cars. No mopeds. Donkeys, cats, and some of the most beautiful harbour architecture in the Mediterranean. Stay the whole day.

Swimming
Bisti Bay ↗ — water taxi · deep sheltered cove · extraordinary clarity · the one
Limnoniza ↗ — quieter alternative · rocky & wild · ringed by pine forest
Dinner
Veranda ↗★ 4.7
Stone steps, terrace over the harbour · sea bream ceviche, Santorini rosé · most romantic in Hydra · reserve ahead
Manna — New Greek ↗★ 4.5
Creative modern cuisine · bold flavours · a meal you'll remember for the cooking
Harbour tip — Monitor VHF Ch 12 from 30 min out. Drop anchor 35–40m off, reverse in stern-to, fenders both sides. First come, first served — arrive before noon.
~10nm · 2hrs
05
Thu 11 – Fri 12 June

Poros → Home

Hydra → Poros → Kalamaki

Sail north from Hydra back to Poros. One more evening ashore, final dinner. Friday: depart 08:00 for the long run home north, Moni island swim stop, back in Kalamaki by late afternoon.

Swimming
Moni Island ↗ — uninhabited nature reserve · wild deer · extraordinary water
Russian Bay, Poros ↗ — revisit at sunset Thursday · even better the second time
Final dinner in Poros
Poseidon ↗★ 4.7
Poros institution · 3,000+ reviews · celebratory last dinner · signal-light service
Taverna Apagio ↗★ 4.8
If you skipped Monday — don't leave Poros without it · moussaka, black risotto, grilled fish
Poros ~20nm · Home ~35nm
